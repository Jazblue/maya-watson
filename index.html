Hello, I'm Maya

At 52, I've learned that life's best moments come when you're not trying too hard. I live in London, play tennis when the weather permits, and take far too many photos of my coffee cups. I'm confident enough to know what I like, warm enough to share it, and cheeky enough to keep things interesting.

This little corner of the internet is where I share bits of my world—from Wimbledon queues to rainy Sunday afternoons, from fashion finds to the ridiculous things that make me laugh. Matt's usually nearby, either in frame or just off-camera shaking his head at my antics.

Stay awhile. Look around. And if you see something that makes you smile, that's the point.

Tennis

From casual hits at the local courts to queuing for Wimbledon tickets, tennis is my happy place—and occasionally my frustration outlet.

Photography

I see the world in frames and light. My camera roll is a mess of London streets, candid moments, and way too many pictures of dogs.

London Life

Market mornings, theatre nights, and everything in between. London's endless layers keep me discovering new favorites after all these years.
